A simple step-by-step guide for people who **fill forms and submit requests**.

If you **approve** other people’s requests, open the [approver guide](/help/e-approval-for-approvers).

---

## What you can do with this guide

1. Open E-Approval  
2. Start and submit a request  
3. Track your request  
4. Fix a request that was sent back  
5. Cancel a request (when allowed)

---

## Before you start

You need:

- Access to your **company workspace**  
- Your login (email + password, or **Sign in with Microsoft**)  
- Permission to use **E-Approval**

Ask IT or your admin for your workspace login address if you are unsure.

---

## Step 1 — Open E-Approval

1. In the left sidebar, click **E-Approval**.  
2. You will mainly use:

| Menu item | What it is for |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Overview** | Quick summary and shortcuts |
| **Submissions** | Your requests and drafts |
| **My E-Approval profile** | Optional: save your signature |

---

## Step 2 — (Optional) Save your signature

Some forms ask you to sign when you submit. Saving a signature once saves time later.

1. Go to **E-Approval → My E-Approval profile**.  
2. Open **My signature**.  
3. Choose **Draw**, **Type**, or **Upload**.  
4. Tick the consent checkbox(es) shown on the page.  
5. Click **Save signature**.

If your form does not ask for a signature, you can skip this step.

---

## Step 3 — Start a new request

1. Go to **E-Approval → Submissions**.  
2. Click **New submission**  
   (or from **Overview**, click **New submission**).  
3. Find the form you need (use search if the list is long).  
4. Click **Start request**.  
5. Fill in all **required** fields (usually marked).  
6. Upload any files the form asks for — wait until each upload finishes.  
7. When everything looks correct, click **Submit request**.

You should see a success message (for example **Submission sent**).

**Write down the document number** shown on the request. Use it if you ask for help.

### Save a draft (finish later)

1. While filling the form, click **Save draft**.  
2. Later: **E-Approval → Submissions** → open the **Draft** → **Continue editing** → **Submit request**.

### If you cannot submit

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Button does nothing / error | Fill all required fields; wait for file uploads to finish |
| Form not in the list | Ask your admin to publish the form or give you access |
| Wrong form started | Leave the draft and start again with the correct form |

---

## Step 4 — Track your request

1. Go to **E-Approval → Submissions**.  
2. Find your request (search by document number if needed).  
3. Click it to open details.

### Status meanings (simple)

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Saved, not sent yet |
| **Pending** | Waiting for an approver |
| **Needs revision** | Approver asked you to fix something |
| **Approved** | Fully approved — done |
| **Rejected** | Stopped — usually start a **new** request if you still need it |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the request |

### Tabs you will see

| Tab | What to look at |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Request** | Your answers and attachments |
| **Approvals** | Who is next, and the approval history |
| **Activity** | Comments and notes |
| **Decide** | Actions such as cancel or resubmit |

**Useful:** Click **Print / PDF** if you need a paper or PDF copy.

---

## Step 5 — If your request was sent back (Needs revision)

1. Open **E-Approval → Submissions**.  
2. Open the request with status **Needs revision**.  
3. Read the **revision remarks** carefully (banner and **Activity** tab).  
4. Choose:

| Action | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Edit and resubmit** | You need to change answers or files |
| **Resubmit without changes** | Nothing to change; send it back into approval |
| **Cancel request** | You no longer need this request |

5. If editing: fix the form → click **Resubmit request**.  
6. Status should return to **Pending**. Wait for the next approval.

---

## Step 6 — Cancel a pending request

While the request is still waiting (**Pending**):

1. Open the request.  
2. Go to the **Decide** tab.  
3. Click **Cancel request** (if shown).  
4. Confirm.

You can also send a **follow-up to approver** from **Decide** if your company allows it (a gentle reminder).

---

## Step 7 — Notifications

1. Click the **bell** icon at the top of the screen.  
2. Open an item to jump to that request.  

You may also get **email** when someone approves, rejects, or returns your request (if your company enabled email).

---

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Open **E-Approval → Submissions → New submission**  
- [ ] **Start request** → fill form → **Submit request**  
- [ ] Note the document number  
- [ ] Check status under **Submissions** (**Pending**)  
- [ ] If **Needs revision** → fix → **Resubmit request**  
- [ ] Wait until status is **Approved**

---

## FAQ

**I do not see E-Approval in the menu.**  
Ask your admin to enable E-Approval and give you requestor access.

**Can I edit after I submit?**  
Not while it is **Pending**, unless an approver uses **Request revision**, or you **Cancel** and start again.

**Who is next?**  
Open the request → **Approvals** tab → look at **Waiting on…** and the workflow path.

**My request was Rejected.**  
You usually cannot reopen it. Start a **new** submission if you still need approval.
