Use these for an all-users announcement that **UAT is live** and testing is ongoing.  
Staging URL: **https://staging.alliancetowers.com**

**Sign-in:** Users must sign in with their **Microsoft work accounts** (forced Microsoft SSO).  
Passkeys / fingerprint can be added later under **My security** after the first Microsoft sign-in.

Copy the version you prefer into Outlook / Teams / Mail, then adjust names and dates.

## Option A — Professional

**Subject:** TowerOS UAT is now live — sign in with your Microsoft account  
**Preview:** Staging is open for UAT. Sign in with Microsoft is required for all users.

---

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that **TowerOS is now live on UAT (User Acceptance Testing)**.

The staging environment is available for all users to validate workflows, forms, approvals, and day-to-day operations before production go-live.

**Access**  
https://staging.alliancetowers.com

**How to sign in**  
Please sign in with your **Microsoft work account**. Choose **Sign in with Microsoft** on the login page. Other sign-in methods are not available for UAT.

After your first sign-in, you may be asked to set up an authenticator app (MFA). Fingerprint / passkey is optional and can be added later under **My security → Passkeys**.

**What we ask of you**
- Open the link above and sign in with your Microsoft account
- Complete any MFA / authenticator steps if prompted
- Test the modules relevant to your role
- Report issues, unclear steps, or missing data to the TowerOS project team
- Note that UAT data may be refreshed or reset during this period

**Important**  
This environment is for **testing only**. Do not treat UAT records as final production records.

Thank you for your participation. Your feedback during this phase is essential to a smooth production launch.

Best regards,  
TowerOS Project Team  
Alliance Towers

Hi everyone,

Quick update: **TowerOS UAT is live.**

**Open staging here:**  
https://staging.alliancetowers.com

**Sign-in**  
Use your **Microsoft work account** — tap **Sign in with Microsoft**. That’s the required way into UAT.

**What to do**
1. Open the link and sign in with Microsoft  
2. Try the screens you use for your job  
3. If something’s confusing or broken, send it to the project team  

Heads-up: this is a **test** environment. Data can change while we keep improving things.

Thanks for helping us get this right before full go-live.

Cheers,  
TowerOS Team
