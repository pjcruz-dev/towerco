# Getting started with TowerOS

TowerOS is your workspace for telecom site operations, approvals, documents, projects, and tickets.

## Prerequisites

- You can sign in to your tenant workspace URL.
- Your account is active.
- You have at least `dashboard:view`.

## Steps

1. Open your tenant URL and sign in.
2. Land on **Dashboard** for a summary of work that needs attention.
3. Use the left sidebar to open modules enabled for your workspace.
4. Open **Notifications** when you need approvals, ticket updates, or rollout alerts.
5. Use search / command palette (`Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K`) to jump to pages or find records.

## Expected result

You can navigate the workspace, see modules you are allowed to use, and open the main operational pages without errors.

## Common errors

- **Blank sidebar groups** — the module may be disabled for your tenant, or you lack permission.
- **Redirected to login** — session expired; sign in again.
- **MFA challenge** — complete multi-factor authentication if your tenant requires it.
