# Sites overview

Sites are the physical or logical locations TowerOS uses as the anchor for documents, towers, fiber, assets, and projects.

## Prerequisites

- Sites module is enabled for your tenant.
- You have `sites:view`.

## Steps

1. Open **Sites** from the sidebar.
2. Search by site code or name.
3. Open a site to view status, type, and linked operational context.
4. Use filters to focus on active or specific site types when available.

## Expected result

You can find a site quickly and open its detail page to continue related work (documents, rollouts, tickets).

## Common errors

- **Sites not in the sidebar** — module disabled or missing `sites:view`.
- **No search results** — check spelling of site code; try a shorter query.
- **Cannot edit site fields** — your role may be view-only.

## Related workflows

- Documents (site binders)
- Project-One rollouts
- Ticketing (link work to a site when relevant)
