A simple step-by-step guide for people who **review and decide** on requests (approve, reject, or ask for changes).

If you only **submit** requests, open the [requestor guide](/help/e-approval-for-requestors).

---

## What you can do with this guide

1. Save your electronic signature (required to approve)  
2. Open your approval inbox  
3. Review a request  
4. Approve, reject, or request revision  

---

## Before you start

You need:

- Access to your **company workspace**  
- Your login (email + password, or **Sign in with Microsoft**)  
- Permission to **approve** in E-Approval  
- A saved **electronic signature** (before you can approve)

Ask IT or your admin for your workspace login address if you are unsure.

**Note:** Passkeys / MFA are only for **signing in**. Approving still needs your **electronic signature**.

---

## Step 1 — Save your signature (do this once)

Approving **requires** a signature. Do this before your first approval.

1. In the left sidebar, open **E-Approval → My E-Approval profile**.  
2. Open **My signature**.  
3. Choose one method:

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Draw** | Draw with mouse or finger. Use **Clear** to redo. |
| **Type** | Type your full name and check the preview. |
| **Upload** | Upload a clear PNG or JPEG (keep it small). |

4. Tick **all** consent checkbox(es) shown (electronic signature / storage consent).  
5. Click **Save signature** and wait for **Signature saved**.

You can change your signature later on the same page.

---

## Step 2 — Open work waiting for you

1. Go to **E-Approval → Approvals**.  
2. Use the **Awaiting me** filter (this is your inbox).  
3. Or open an item from:
   - Header **Notifications** (bell)  
   - **Overview → Needs my approval**  

Page title is usually **Approval inbox**.  
If empty: **Nothing awaiting you**.

---

## Step 3 — Review the request carefully

1. Click **Open submission** on a row.  
2. On the **Request** tab:
   - Read the answers  
   - Open **Attachments** and check files (preview / download)  
3. On the **Approvals** tab, check:
   - **Workflow path** — steps in the chain  
   - **Waiting on…** — you should see **You** on your step  
   - **Approval trail** — earlier decisions  
4. On the **Activity** tab, read comments or return remarks.

**Do not approve** until you are comfortable with the form answers and attachments.

---

## Step 4 — Make your decision

Go to the **Decide** tab → **Your decision**.

| Button | When to use | What you must provide |
|--------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Approve** | Everything is OK | Signature + consent. Remarks optional. |
| **Reject** | Stop this request | **Remarks** required (explain why). |
| **Request revision** | Needs fixes; they can edit and resubmit | **Remarks** required (say what to change). |

### Approve

1. Confirm **Your signature** (from your profile, or Draw / Type / Upload now).  
2. Tick the consent checkbox(es).  
3. Optional remarks.  
4. Click **Approve**.

**If you see an error**

| Message | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Add your signature before approving | Draw, Type, or Upload a signature, then try again |
| Accept … consent before approving | Tick the consent checkbox(es), then approve |

### Reject

1. Type clear remarks (why it is rejected).  
2. Click **Reject**.  

The request stops. The requestor is notified. They usually must start a **new** request if they still need approval.

### Request revision (send back for fixes)

1. Type clear remarks (what is missing or wrong).  
2. Optional: **Require full re-approval** — after they resubmit, the whole chain starts again (when available).  
3. Click **Request revision**.

The requestor fixes the form and resubmits. It may return to you or continue from a later step, depending on the form rules.

---

## Step 5 — After you decide

- Status updates on the request.  
- The requestor is notified (in-app and email when configured).  
- If more approvers are needed, the next person in the chain is notified.  
- Your inbox (**Awaiting me**) removes that item once you have acted.

You can reopen the request anytime from **Approvals** (**All**) or by searching the document number.

---

## Step 6 — Notifications

1. Click the **bell** icon in the top header.  
2. Open an item to jump straight to the request.  

Email may also arrive when a new request is waiting for you (if enabled by your company).

---

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Save signature on **My E-Approval profile**  
- [ ] Open **E-Approval → Approvals** (**Awaiting me**)  
- [ ] Open the request → review **Request** + attachments  
- [ ] Check **Approvals** (workflow / waiting on you)  
- [ ] **Decide** → **Approve** (or Reject / Request revision)  
- [ ] Confirm the item leaves your inbox

## Parallel approvals (if you see them)

Some steps say things like:

- “Any one can approve — first approval continues”  
- “All listed approvers must approve”  
- “At least N of M…”

Follow the rule shown on screen. If you are unsure, ask your process owner or admin.

## FAQ

**I see Submissions but not Approvals.**  
You may only have requestor access. Ask your admin for approve permission.

**I approved but it still says Pending.**  
Another step may still be waiting. Open **Approvals** tab → **Waiting on…** / workflow path.

**Someone else should approve instead of me.**  
Ask an admin — they can reroute on some requests.

**Do I need a passkey to approve?**  
No. Passkeys are for login only. Approving needs **Your signature** and consent.

**Can I leave a comment without deciding?**  
Yes — use the **Activity** tab → **Add a comment**. Official decisions must use **Decide**.
